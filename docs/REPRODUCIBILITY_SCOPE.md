# Reproducibility Scope

This repository is a compact manuscript companion, not a mirror of the full
historical research workspace.

Included:

- the audited strict-v2 Proposed implementation;
- the frozen paper configuration;
- configurable data, training, and frozen-evaluation entry points;
- protocol, implementation, accounting, and source-integrity tests;
- compact canonical artifacts added after result verification.

Excluded:

- raw market data and redistributed vendor downloads;
- model checkpoints and large prediction arrays;
- exploratory, failed, and dated ablation orchestration;
- complete third-party baseline repositories;
- local paths, Docker layers, logs, and Optuna databases.

The source manifest records hashes of the audited runtime. Third-party methods
must be obtained from their original repositories and licenses.
